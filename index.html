Not a test. Not a form.
A gentle, private conversation — at your own pace, in your own words.

What this is
RESILIENCE asks you about your everyday life — how you get around, how you are feeling, who you have around you. It takes around ten to fifteen minutes, and you can go as slowly as you like.
What this is not
This is not a medical test. No decisions will be made about you based on what you share here. There are no right or wrong answers — only your experience, in your own words.
Your information is yours
Nothing is saved and nothing is shared without your permission. At the end of the conversation, you choose exactly what happens with what you have told us. You are in complete control of that.
What you will receive
Whatever you decide to share, you will receive a personal Wellness Guide — yours to keep. It is based entirely on what you have told us, and is there to help you think about what might support you to live well.

You can stop at any point. There is no pressure and no obligation.
